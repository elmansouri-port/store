All the best features, in one simple place

Team messaging Your team conversations organized by project, client, or topic — no more endless threads in your inbox. Every exchange is archived, searchable, and accessible to everyone involved.

Screen sharing Show exactly what you want, in one click, during any Rainbow audio or video call. No more describing what you see — just share it. Perfect for project reviews, client presentations, and remote support.

Text formatting

Write structured messages with headings, lists, code blocks, and formatted text — for clear briefs, readable minutes, and instructions that are understood the first time.

Video call transcripts

No more note-taking during your calls. Rainbow automatically transcribes every video conversation and attaches it directly to the matching conversation thread. Find any exchange again in seconds, even months later.

Meeting analytics

Measure each participant's speaking time, your teams' engagement, and the actual length of your meetings. Concrete data to help you organize your next meetings better.
