Your data, protected & hosted in France

Rainbow is hosted in France, operated under French law, certified by ANSSI, and HDS-compliant for health data. No Cloud Act. No access possible by foreign authorities. It's the only guarantee that matters when your communications are confidential.

ANSSI CSPN

Rainbow™ has obtained the First Level Security Certification (CSPN), issued by ANSSI, France's highest security standard. Only three communications solutions have obtained it, and Rainbow is the largest of the three.

HDS

The HDS certification (Hébergement de Données de Santé, or Health Data Hosting) is a mandatory French certification that guarantees a provider hosting health data meets strict requirements for security, confidentiality, and the protection of medical data.

BSI C5

The BSI C5 standard, published by Germany's Federal Office for Information Security (BSI), establishes a baseline security standard for cloud services and helps customers assess, manage, and oversee their cloud service providers.

GDPR

Rainbow services are designed to comply with the European directive on data protection, which strengthens the protection of privacy and personal data.

ISO-27001 & SOC

The data centers rely on modern technologies certified to ISO-27001 and SOC standards. They are enclosed by barbed-wire fencing, and physical access is strictly monitored 24×7.

HIPAA

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) is a US certification that guarantees the security of healthcare-related data.

FERPA

FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) is a US law that protects the confidentiality of students' school records and gives them the right to access, correct, and control the disclosure of their educational information.

ACN

A national cybersecurity certification issued by Italy's National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN) to certify that ICT products and services comply with the security requirements set out in Italian law.

ENS

A certification issued under the Spanish national security framework, aimed at protecting information systems against internal and external cyber threats and incidents.
